Trigonometric functions, in multiples of \(\pi\)#

Auxiliary function \(\mathrm{sinpi}(x) = \sin(\pi x)\)#

ctx.sinpi(x)#

where ctx is math53, mathc53, ctxcpp, ctxflint.

Returns the sine of \(x \cdot \pi\). See also BoostMath [111], Ehrhardt [297] (4.2.57), Flint [745], Flint [735], Mpmath [556].

The function can also be expressed as

\[\sin\!\left(\pi z\right) = \frac{\pi}{\Gamma(z) \Gamma\!\left(1 - z\right)}\]
